How to tidy a town

1

Find the bricks

Walk up to the labelled brick piles. Look at a brick and press E to pick it up.

2

Follow the blueprint

Glowing blue shapes show where each brick belongs. Match the shape and color, then press E to place it.

3

Watch it rise

Every brick brings a building back to life. Finish all nine to restore the whole town.

4

Unlock helpful skills

Sprint, double jump, a brick magnet and a bigger wheelbarrow arrive as the town grows. Open the plan with B.
